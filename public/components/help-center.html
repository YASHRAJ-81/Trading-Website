To bind your bank card:
  1. Go to the "Account" section in the app.
  2. Tap "Payment Methods".
  3. Select "Add Bank Card" and enter your card details.
  4. Verify with the OTP sent to your phone/email.
  5. Your card will be securely linked to your account.
To reset your password:
  • Click on the "Forgot Password?" link on the login screen.
  • Enter your registered email or phone number.
  • Check your inbox or SMS for a reset code.
  • Follow the link and create a new password.
  • Log in again using the new password.
Follow these security tips:
  • Use strong, unique passwords for each account.
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) whenever possible.
  • Don’t share your login credentials with anyone.
  • Be cautious of phishing emails or suspicious links.
  • Always log out from public devices.
